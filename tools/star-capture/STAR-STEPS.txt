Star button capture, using Launchpad's own read-only port kit (built 2026-10-29).
It installs nothing. For 30 seconds it records every button the head unit sees,
with the device it came from and its key code, into launchpad-port-report.txt
on the stick. We use it to learn how the two star buttons (steering wheel, and
the one on the dash panel under the screen) reach the head unit, and what a
long press of each does.

ON THE COMPUTER
1. On the USB drive, delete NEMESIS.data and qi-write.sh (and any older
   NEMESIS.sh). The qi-capture / qi-read / qi-write folders can stay.
2. Copy NEMESIS.data, README.txt and the whole launchpad-port folder from this
   folder to the ROOT of the drive.
3. Eject.

IN THE CAR
1. Park, car on, screen up. In Setup > Button, note what each star button is
   currently set to, then set BOTH to None so a short press does nothing.
2. Insert the drive in the data USB port, tap Yes within 60 seconds.
3. Right after Yes, count to five, then:
   PHASE A, steering wheel star:
     - press and release, pause two seconds, repeat so you do three presses
     - then hold it for about three seconds and release
     - watch the screen while holding: note anything that opens or changes
   Pause five seconds.
   PHASE B, dash panel star (the one under the screen):
     - three presses two seconds apart, then one three-second hold
     - again note what the screen does during the hold
4. Wait for the success dialog (about 30 to 40 seconds after Yes). If the
   dialog comes before you finish phase B, that is fine; note where you got to.
5. Pull the drive.

WHAT COMES BACK
launchpad-port-report.txt at the root of the drive. Keep it, and note what each
star was set to before and what the screen did during each long hold.
